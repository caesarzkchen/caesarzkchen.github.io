Generative AI & efficient computation

Zhuokun Chen.

Making generative models
more efficient.

I am Zhuokun Chen, and I also go by Caesar. I received my master's degree from South China University of Technology in 2025, supervised by Mingkui Tan. In the same year, I began my PhD at Monash University, supervised by Bohan Zhuang and Jianfei Cai.

My research focuses on improving the efficiency of generative models, including image and video generation models and large language models (LLMs). I am particularly interested in sparse and linear attention, token reduction, and related techniques.

Contact: caesard216@gmail.com
